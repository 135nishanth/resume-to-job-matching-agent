Job Title: Senior AI Backend Engineer
Company: Apex Intelligent Systems
Location: San Francisco, CA (Hybrid / Remote)
Experience Level: 5+ years

ABOUT THE ROLE
We are seeking an experienced Senior AI Backend Engineer to lead the design and development of our core agentic platform. You will build mission-critical RESTful APIs, high-throughput asynchronous services, and vector search pipelines connecting modern Large Language Models to enterprise knowledge graphs.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design, build, and maintain robust, high-performance REST APIs and microservices using Python and FastAPI.
- Architect scalable database schemas with PostgreSQL and implement high-efficiency vector embeddings retrieval using pgvector.
- Build reliable asynchronous background worker jobs and distributed queuing pipelines using Celery and Redis.
- Deploy, monitor, and scale cloud infrastructure on AWS utilizing Docker containers and CI/CD automation.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers and product managers to deliver seamless AI agent features.

REQUIRED SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS
- 5+ years of software development experience with Python.
- Proven expertise in building production REST APIs using FastAPI or Django.
- Strong relational database experience with PostgreSQL, including schema optimization and indexing.
- Hands-on experience with Docker containerization and AWS cloud services (EC2, S3, RDS).
- Familiarity with Git, automated testing (Pytest), and CI/CD pipelines.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related technical field.

PREFERRED SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS
- Experience with AI frameworks such as LangChain, LlamaIndex, or OpenAI APIs.
- Experience with vector databases and semantic search embedding models.
- Knowledge of Go or distributed messaging systems (Kafka, RabbitMQ).
- Experience with Kubernetes (k8s) cluster orchestration and Terraform.
